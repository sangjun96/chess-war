# Royal Calamity effect assets

The king's `royal_calamity` attack layers a lightning strike, fire bomb, epic explosion,
and impact burst. The `epic-explosion.png` and `impact-burst.png` sprites are from
**Super Pixel Effects Gigapack** by Will Tice / unTied Games; see
[`../License.txt`](../License.txt) for its attribution and distribution terms.

`crown-lightning.png` and `crown-firebomb.png` are from the supplied **Magic Pack 9**
by Luis Zuno. Keep its supplied license with any redistribution of the game.
